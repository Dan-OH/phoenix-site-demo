TOURISM SASKATCHEWAN

Land of the
Living Skies.

A destination campaign that invites travellers to see Saskatchewan in a new light.

Project overview+

THE BRIEF

Tourism Saskatchewan wanted to attract more visitors, build pride among residents and change how people saw the province as a travel destination. Phoenix Group helped launch a destination brand and a long-term campaign that showcased what makes Saskatchewan distinct.

ExploreSask began in the Grasslands, with films about the night sky and the warmth of local communities. Later work featured camping and paddling in the north, followed by a look at Saskatchewan’s cities and hidden gems for younger travellers.

CLIENT

SERVICES

The challenge

Tourism Saskatchewan wanted to attract visitors and build pride at home by broadening perceptions of the province.

Our approach

Phoenix developed a brand platform and a series of films that explored the province through its night skies, communities, northern landscapes and cities.

The impact

The campaign gave Tourism Saskatchewan one platform for showcasing a wide range of experiences across channels.
