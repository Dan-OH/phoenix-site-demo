A&W CANADA

A&W Brand

Refresh.

Bringing A&W’s iconic orange and craveable food back to the centre of its brand.

Project overview+

THE BRIEF

A&W had built a familiar Canadian brand around its burgers and root beer. Over time, its emphasis on natural ingredients became less distinctive as competitors made similar claims. Partnerships and sub-brands had also introduced different colours, illustration styles and assets, making the brand harder to recognize from one touchpoint to the next.

We brought the focus back to the food. The refreshed identity puts A&W orange front and centre, pairs a custom type family with modular shapes inspired by burger ingredients, and gives food photography a more prominent role. The Burger Family and the Great Root Bear were redrawn in a shared illustration style.

The system extends across advertising, restaurant signage, packaging and the digital experience. Rethink reports a 15% increase in advertising campaign recall following the refresh.

The challenge

A&W’s focus on ingredient claims had lost its distinctiveness as competitors followed. Its brand assets also varied across teams and touchpoints.

Our approach

We revived A&W orange, built a shape system from burger ingredients, and introduced custom type. It also unified the illustrations and refreshed the food photography.

The impact

The identity rolled out across advertising, signage, packaging and digital. Rethink reports a 15% increase in campaign recall.
